# Domain Glossary

## Staff User

A person who operates the bookstore through the internal system. A Staff User has roles and permissions.

## Customer

A buyer profile identified by phone number. A Customer owns orders and loyalty points but is not a Staff User.

## Guest Checkout

A purchase made without a Customer login. The buyer's phone number finds or creates the Customer profile.

## Storefront Order

An order placed by a Customer through the public sales channel. Stock is reserved when the order is accepted.
